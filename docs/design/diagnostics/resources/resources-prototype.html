How to read this / captured evidence

Mark before open/switch/close, then compare after disposal settles. Expected inactive readers can remain for five minutes. Positive deltas are investigation hints, not leak proof.

PDF task owners are not actual worker-thread counts. Registered zero and no registered owner describe different coverage. JS heap comes from nonstandard performance.memory where supported; it is not process or GPU memory. Do not add heap, canvas, Blob and preview estimates: they overlap.
