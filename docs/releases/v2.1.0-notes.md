# v2.1.0 — A new welcome to your reading space

PaperTrail now shows a lightweight, book-themed loading screen while your reading workspace starts.

## What's new

- A responsive book-flip animation appears early, with progress based on completed application startup milestones.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected, and startup failures offer actionable retry feedback.
- Saved-library information is restored separately from file-permission renewal and document reopening.
- Production and staging have no artificial loading delay. Numbered previews include a seven-second animation review hold.

## Delivery improvements

- Refined staging integration and reviewed release promotion, with exact candidate identities, checksum verification and channel-specific URLs.
- Documentation-only staging merges can preserve a pending validated application candidate without accepting stale artifacts.
- PDF/EPUB reload tests wait for workspace readiness before reselecting documents.
- Visual QA captures retain source/state evidence; adoption of reviewed regression baselines remains separately tracked.
